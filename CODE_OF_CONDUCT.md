# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a roundhouse-kick-free experience for everyone.
(Metaphorically. Chuck Norris reserves the right to roundhouse kick bad code.)

## Our Standards

✅ Be respectful and constructive  
✅ Welcome newcomers — even those who don't know Chuck Norris  
✅ Give helpful feedback  
✅ Accept that Chuck Norris is always right  

❌ No harassment, discrimination, or trolling  
❌ No submitting code that would disappoint Chuck Norris  

## Enforcement

Violations will be handled by Chuck Norris personally.
You don't want that.
